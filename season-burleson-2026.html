Alec Burleson for the win

With a hat tip to the RISP who made it possible

How did Alec Burleson lead the majors in RBI? He came to the plate with men on base more than any other qualified hitter, and in 2026 he hit much better with them on.

Burleson drove in 116 runs in 2026. Baltimore’s Pete Alonso was next in the majors, with 113. Sal Stewart of the Reds was second in the National League, with 112. Teammate Jordan Walker was seventh in the majors, with 102.1

Burleson is the first Cardinal to lead the majors in RBI since Joe Torre drove in 137 in 1971. He is the first to lead the National League since Albert Pujols drove in 118 in 2010.2

Burleson bats left-handed. The last left-handed Cardinal to lead the majors was Enos Slaughter, with 130 in 1946. The last to lead the National League was Stan Musial, with 109 in 1956. Burleson’s 116 are the most by a left-handed Cardinal since Musial drove in 126 in 1954.2

His bat alone does not explain the title. His .816 OPS tied for 34th among the 135 qualified hitters in the majors, and tied for 21st among the 68 in the National League.3

Burleson batted with men on base in 54% of his plate appearances, the highest share of any qualified hitter. The typical qualified hitter batted with men on 44% of the time.4

That share is high even for an RBI leader. Since 2000, the typical league or major-league RBI leader batted with men on 49% of the time. The last one with a higher share than Burleson’s was Alex Rodriguez in 2007, at 56%.5

Burleson batted third in 119 of his 150 starts. Ahead of him, JJ Wetherholt led off in 135 starts and Iván Herrera batted second in 147. Both got on base more often than the typical qualified hitter: .339 and .353, against .331.6

A typical qualified hitter’s OPS rose .040 with men on. Burleson’s rose .159, the 22nd-biggest rise among the 135. His rise was smaller in 2024 and 2025, about .095 each year.4

By our count of the play-by-play, about 486 runners were on base when he batted. He drove in 90 of them, nearly one in five, the best rate of the eight Cardinals with 300 or more plate appearances. Jordan Walker was next, at about 16%. Burleson’s other nine RBI with men on were his own, on home runs, and 17 more came on solo home runs.7

Left-handed pitchers

Most of his damage came against right-handers. Burleson had a .939 OPS against them in 464 plate appearances and a .549 OPS against left-handers in 206. He drove in 76 runs against right-handers and 40 against left-handers.8

Home and road

He also hit far better on the road (.890 OPS) than at Busch Stadium (.739).8 Busch played low in 2026. Games there produced about 13% fewer runs than the Cardinals’ road games, the fourth-lowest rate in the majors.9 But the other Cardinals hitters’ gap was much smaller, .652 at home and .697 on the road.10 And Burleson had hit better at home in each of the three seasons before.8 The park explains some of his split. We could not explain the rest.

What we checked and set aside

RBI depend on the hitters who get on ahead of Burleson. This piece shows the chances he had and what he did with them. One season with men on base is a small sample, and we did not test why he hit better with men on.

Sources and methods

1 MLB’s official data, the Stats API: https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/stats/leaders?leaderCategories=runsBattedIn&season=2026&sportId=1&statGroup=hitting gives the major-league leaders; add &leagueId=104 for the National League.

2 The Lahman database (free from SABR), 1901 through 2025: in the Batting table, add each player’s RBI for a season across all his teams, then take the most in each league and in the majors. Batting side is the bats column in the People table; a switch-hitter is listed as B and is not counted as left-handed. Every leader is also in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-rbi-leaders-men-on.csv.

3 https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/stats?stats=season&group=hitting&season=2026&sportIds=1&gameType=R&playerPool=Qualified&limit=1000 gives every qualified hitter’s season line; a qualified hitter has at least 502 plate appearances. There were 135, 68 of them in the National League. Ties share a rank. All 135 are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-qualified-hitters-2026.csv.

4 The same request with stats=statSplits&sitCodes=ron,risp,r0 gives each qualified hitter’s lines with men on base, with runners in scoring position and with the bases empty. Men on base means at least one runner on when the plate appearance began. The rise is OPS with men on minus OPS with the bases empty. "Typical" is the middle of the 135. Burleson’s earlier seasons come from https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/people/676475/stats?stats=statSplits&group=hitting&season=2025&sitCodes=ron,r0 (change the season). The splits are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-qualified-hitters-2026.csv.

5 Retrosheet play-by-play: for each league and major-league RBI leader, every plate appearance and whether a runner was on first, second or third when it began. A season counts when the play-by-play covers at least 97% of his plate appearances in the Lahman database. That leaves every leader season since 2000 and 249 of 264 overall. Each leader’s counts are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-rbi-leaders-men-on.csv. The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at www.retrosheet.org.

6 https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/game/{gamePk}/boxscore for every Cardinals game lists each batter’s battingOrder: the spot times 100, so a start in the third spot is 300. The on-base percentages are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-qualified-hitters-2026.csv; the typical qualified hitter’s is the middle of the 135.

7 https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/game/{gamePk}/playByPlay for every Cardinals game lists the runners on base after each play. The runners on when Burleson batted are those after the play before his in the same half-inning, plus the automatic runner on second for the first batter of an extra inning. Plays that are not plate appearances, such as a runner caught stealing, are left out. His 670 plate appearances, with 486 runners on and 116 RBI, are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-plate-appearances-2026.csv. Runners driven in are RBI minus his own run on each of his 26 home runs: 90.

8 https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/people/676475/stats?stats=statSplits&group=hitting&season=2026&sitCodes=vl,vr,h,a gives his lines against left- and right-handed pitchers and at home and on the road. Change the season for 2023 through 2025.

9 From the final scores in https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule?sportId=1&season=2026&gameType=R&hydrate=linescore: for each team, runs by both teams per game at home divided by the same on the road. In 81 games at Busch the Cardinals scored 310 and allowed 373; in 81 road games they scored 410 and allowed 376. That ratio is 0.869, the 4th-lowest of 30. Every team is in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-home-road-runs-2026.csv.

10 Every Cardinals hitter’s line in every 2026 boxscore (https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/game/{gamePk}/boxscore), added up at home and on the road, leaving out Burleson. OPS is on-base percentage plus slugging percentage. The sums are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/burleson-teammates-home-road-2026.csv.
