<div style="font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0B1C33;line-height:1.3;margin:0 0 4px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Cardinals couldn't keep it close</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;color:#707070;margin:0 0 14px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">In 2026 there was no one with more losses in one-run games</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;"><b>Were the 2026 Cardinals unlucky?</b> They lost 30 games by one run in 2026, more than any other team in the majors.<sup>1</sup> The Cardinals had not lost that many since 1998.<sup>2</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">They lost so many mainly because they played so many.<sup>3</sup> The Cardinals played 54 one-run games in 2026, tied with the Dodgers for the most in the majors. Eight teams had a worse one-run winning percentage.<sup>1</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Their runs say the record was about right. The Cardinals scored 720 runs in 2026 and allowed 749. A standard formula turns runs into an expected record, and it gave them about 78 wins. They won 77.<sup>4</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Better teams win more close games than worse teams do, but not many more. The way the Cardinals played their other games, they would be expected to go about 27-27 in 54 one-run games. They went 24-30, a normal miss.<sup>5</sup></p>

<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals were outscored mostly in lopsided games.<sup>6</sup> Flip one close loss to a win, and the record moves a full game. The run totals barely move.</p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Early luck, late luck</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Through May 2026, the Cardinals went 31-26. Their runs predicted about 27 wins. From June on, they went 46-59, when their runs predicted about 51. The early luck and the late luck nearly canceled.<sup>7</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Most of that swing came in one-run games: 11-5 through May, 13-25 after.<sup>7</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The bullpen question</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">In 2026, 13 of the Cardinals' 30 one-run losses included a blown save. Five of their 24 one-run wins did, too.<sup>8</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Riley O'Brien saved 38 games in 2026. He blew 5 saves in games the Cardinals lost by one run, and 4 of those came after June.<sup>8</sup> Over the full season, the Cardinals' record matched their runs within one win.<sup>4</sup> From June on, it fell about five wins short of them.<sup>7</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Around the league</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals won about one game fewer than their runs predicted. Thirteen teams missed by more in 2026.<sup>9</sup> The Brewers, who won the National League pennant,<sup>10</sup> won 103 games in 2026, just what their runs predicted. They went 27-20 in one-run games.<sup>9</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">What carries over</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">A losing record tends to repeat the next season. A losing record in close games mostly does not.<sup>11</sup></p>
<div style="background-color:#F4F4F4;border:1px solid #E0E0E0;border-left:4px solid #0B1C33;border-radius:4px;padding:12px 16px;margin:4px 0 14px;">

<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">A team with a losing record was more than twice as likely as a winning team to have a losing record the next season. In one-run games, the gap nearly closes. Falling short of what the runs predicted barely carries over, either.<sup>11</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Losing close games does follow losing in general. Since 1901, 62% of teams with a losing record in their other games also lost more one-run games than they won. Among teams with a winning record in their other games, 35% did.<sup>12</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">From 1901 through 2025, there were 576 team-seasons in which a club lost at least six more one-run games than it won and played again the next season. That next season, those clubs won 48.1% of their one-run games. They won 47.0% of all their games, still a little below average. Only 39% had a winning one-run record.<sup>13</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Before 2026, the Cardinals had 22 such seasons. In 11 of them, the next season brought a winning one-run record. The 2023 Cardinals went 17-26 in one-run games. In 2024 they went 29-22. That year they won 83 games, though their runs predicted about 76.<sup>2</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">What we checked and set aside</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals went 9-10 in extra-inning games in 2026.<sup>14</sup> A second common version of the expected-wins formula also gives them about 78 wins.<sup>4</sup> We did not test whether the bullpen, the lineup or any decision caused the close losses.</p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Sources and methods</div>
<p style="font-size:11px;color:#707070;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 4px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;"><sup>1</sup> MLB's official data, the Stats API: https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule?sportId=1&amp;season=2026&amp;gameType=R&amp;hydrate=linescore gives the final score of all 2,429 regular-season games, and https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams?sportId=1&amp;season=2026 gives each team's league. The game list reproduces every team's 2026 record. A one-run game is any game decided by one run, extra innings included. There were 668. The Cardinals went 24-30 in theirs. No other team lost more than 29. The Dodgers also played 54. A one-run winning percentage is one-run wins divided by one-run games: .444 for the Cardinals. Every team's record is in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/onerun-teams-2026.csv.</p>
<p style="font-size:11px;color:#707070;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 4px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;"><sup>2</sup> History comes from Retrosheet's teamstats file. Each team-season is joined to the Teams table in the Lahman database (free from SABR) on teamIDretro and yearID. That table gives the team's name, its league (lgID) and its official record (W and L).
