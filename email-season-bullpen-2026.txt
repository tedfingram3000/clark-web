<div style="font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0B1C33;line-height:1.3;margin:0 0 4px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Bullpen usage tops franchise history</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;color:#707070;margin:0 0 14px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Cardinals follow the pack and rely on relievers</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals' bullpen threw 613&#8532; innings in 2026, the most in franchise history. The rotation threw 829 innings.<sup>1</sup> No Cardinals rotation had thrown fewer in a full season since at least 1950. Both old marks belonged to the 2021 team.<sup>2</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The record says more about baseball than about this team. Twelve teams used their bullpens more in 2026.<sup>3</sup> The Cardinals also gave their relievers a smaller share of the work than the league did. They have done that every full season since 2008.<sup>4</sup> What set this team apart was a rotation that rarely went deep.</p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Thirteenth in the majors</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The old franchise mark was 584&#8531; relief innings, set in 2021.<sup>2</sup> Giovanny Gallegos led that bullpen with 80&#8531; innings in 73 games. That team won 90 games.<sup>5</sup> The 2026 bullpen passed its mark by 29&#8531; innings.<sup>2</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The 2026 total ranked 13th in the majors. The average big-league bullpen threw about 619 innings. The White Sox led at 766. The Dodgers (556) and Phillies (555) had the two lightest bullpen workloads in the National League.<sup>3</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Brewers, who won the division at 103-59 and carry the NL pennant into the World Series this week, used their bullpen more: 640&#8532; innings with a 3.40 ERA.<sup>6</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The league moved the same way</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Relievers' share of big-league innings has grown for decades. In 1950, relievers threw about a quarter of all innings. By 2026, they threw 43%.<sup>4</sup></p>

<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals followed the league up. They also stayed a step behind it. The 2026 season was the 18th straight full season in which the Cardinals' relief share sat below the league's. The last time it ran above the league was 2007.<sup>4</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The six heaviest bullpen seasons in Cardinals history have all come since 2018.<sup>2</sup> From 1950 through 2025, 70 big-league teams topped 613&#8532; relief innings in a season. Only one of them, the 2012 Rockies, came before 2018.<sup>2</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals had company in 2026. Eight teams set franchise records for relief innings. The White Sox, Nationals and Astros broke theirs by the widest margins.<sup>7</sup> Fifteen teams did it in 2019, the most in any season since 1951. Before 2026, 28 of the 30 franchise records had been set in 2018 or later.<sup>7</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Where the innings came from</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The Cardinals had 39 starts of fewer than five innings in 2026. That is fewer than the 2021 team (50) or the 2022 team (48).<sup>8</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The shortfall came at the other end. Cardinals starters went six innings or more 58 times. That ties 2021 for the fewest by a Cardinals rotation in a full season since 1950. Another 65 starts lasted at least five innings but fewer than six. The average start lasted just over five innings.<sup>8</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Michael McGreevy carried most of it. He made 31 starts and threw 168&#8532; innings, the most on the staff. Andre Pallante threw 162 innings in 29 starts, and Matthew Liberatore threw 155 in 31.<sup>1</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">How it pitched</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The rotation was about average. Cardinals starters posted a 4.17 ERA in 2026. All big-league starters posted a 4.24.<sup>9</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The bullpen ran behind the league. Cardinals relievers posted a 4.40 ERA. Big-league relievers as a group posted a 4.09.<sup>9</sup> The Cardinals finished with 30 blown saves and 46 saves.<sup>1</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Gordon Graceffo threw the most relief innings on the team: 78, in 61 games, with a 2.77 ERA.<sup>1</sup> In 2025 he made 26 relief appearances.<sup>5</sup> Justin Bruihl pitched in a team-high 73 games and threw 77&#8532; innings.<sup>1</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Who was out there</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Twenty-four pitchers worked in relief for the 2026 Cardinals. The top five threw 348&#8531; of the 613&#8532; relief innings: Graceffo, Bruihl, George Soriano, Riley O'Brien and Ryne Stanek.<sup>1</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Some of the 2025 bullpen came back. Eleven pitchers made 20 or more relief appearances for the 2025 Cardinals. Six of them relieved for the club again in 2026. A seventh, Kyle Leahy, moved to the rotation. He made 30 starts.<sup>5</sup></p>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">The 2026 bullpen made 549 appearances.<sup>1</sup> The 2021 bullpen made 556.<sup>2</sup> The 2026 relievers set the record by staying in longer each time out.</p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">What we checked and set aside</div>
<p style="font-size:13px;color:#2D3640;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 10px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">We tested whether the workload wore the bullpen down as the season went on. The September numbers argue against it. Cardinals relievers posted a 3.92 ERA in September, their second-best month. Their worst months were March/April (5.15) and August (5.09).<sup>10</sup></p>
<div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:2px;text-transform:uppercase;color:#0B1C33;font-weight:700;margin:16px 0 10px;padding-bottom:5px;border-bottom:2px solid #0B1C33;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;">Sources and methods</div>
<p style="font-size:11px;color:#707070;line-height:1.5;margin:0 0 4px;font-family:'Helvetica Neue',Helvetica,Arial,sans-serif;"><sup>1</sup> MLB's official data, the Stats API: https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule?sportId=1&amp;teamId=138&amp;season=2026&amp;gameType=R&amp;hydrate=linescore lists all 162 Cardinals games, and https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/game/{gamePk}/boxscore gives each pitcher's line in each game. The pitcher who started a game (gamesStarted) is the starter. Every other pitcher that day made a relief appearance, so a relief appearance is any game a pitcher entered after the start. Relief innings are the outs those pitchers recorded, divided by three. Cardinals relievers recorded 1,841 outs (613&#8532; innings) in 549 appearances by 24 pitchers. Starters recorded 2,487 outs (829 innings). The top five relievers recorded 1,045 outs. Saves and blown saves are MLB's own counts in the same boxscores. Every pitcher's starts, relief games, innings, earned runs, saves and blown saves are in https://data.700clark.com/season-2026/data/bullpen-cardinals-pitchers-2026.csv. Innings there are in baseball notation (613.2 is 613&#8532;).</p>
